## Agreement 1 — Residential Membership Agreement + Auto-Renewal Disclosure

### Purpose
Govern the recurring relationship for Essential ($39/mo or $420/yr), Plus ($109/mo or $1,180/yr), and Concierge ($209/mo or $2,260/yr) tiers, including auto-renewal mechanics, use-it-or-lose-it included hours, member pricing, and cancellation.

### Parties
- **Contractor:** La Vaca General Contractors, LLC (HIC #13VH13373800)
- **Member:** Residential homeowner

### Term Structure
Month-to-month or annual; auto-renews unless cancelled per the cancellation clause.

### Document Structure (Recommended Outline)
1. Parties, recitals, definitions
2. **Conspicuous Auto-Renewal Disclosure block** (top of document, bold/all-caps, signed acknowledgment)
3. NJ HIC notices (3-day rescission, do-not-sign-in-blank, contractor registration info) — pull from CUST-contract Section "NOTICE TO CONSUMER"
4. Mechanics' Lien Notice — pull from CUST-contract Section "NOTICE TO OWNER - MECHANICS' LIEN RIGHTS"
5. Membership tier elected + Exhibit A (covered services and exclusions, by reference to service one-pager)
6. Included hours, use-it-or-lose-it acknowledgment, no-rollover acknowledgment
7. Pricing: monthly/annual rate, after-hours premium ($245/hr), materials markup (retail + 25%), member discount on additional work
8. Payment terms: card-on-file authorization, billing cadence, failed-payment cure period, late fee
9. Auto-renewal: term length, advance reminder (30–60 day for annual), cancellation method
10. Cancellation: how, when effective, refund policy with statutory carve-out
11. "Not a regulated home inspection" disclaimer (covers Annual Safety Audit, Sump Test, Spring/Fall visits)
12. ADA and environmental disclaimers (Aging-in-Place; mold/lead/asbestos/radon)
13. Property eligibility limits (single-family, under 6,000 SF, single property per contract, etc. — pull from menu's "Property Size Limits" section)
14. Member service standards (response times by tier)
15. Limitation of liability with explicit Consumer Fraud Act carve-out (Section 8 of CUST-contract is the model)
16. Warranty: workmanship only, 30-day on handyman repairs, no warranty on inspection-only services, manufacturer pass-through
17. Indemnification (mutual)
18. Insurance representation (CGL, bond, workers' comp status)
19. Dispute resolution: good-faith negotiation → mediation → NJ court (Essex County); class-action waiver pending Decision #4
20. Force majeure
21. Notices
22. Governing law (NJ)
23. Entire agreement, severability, plain language, etc.
24. Signatures + signed Auto-Renewal Acknowledgment

### Must-Have Clauses (NJ Statute Anchors)

| Clause | Statute |
|---|---|
| HIC # on face | N.J.S.A. 56:8-149 |
| 3-day rescission notice (bold/caps) | N.J.S.A. 56:8-152; N.J.A.C. 13:45A-16.2(a)(12) |
| Auto-renewal: term, price, cancellation method, conspicuous | N.J.S.A. 56:12-16 |
| 30–60 day advance reminder (annual plans) | N.J.S.A. 56:12-17 (confirm exact requirement) |
| Use-it-or-lose-it acknowledgment | N.J.S.A. 56:8-1 (CFA omission risk) |
| After-hours rate disclosed upfront | N.J.A.C. 13:45A-16.2(a)(7) |
| Materials markup disclosed upfront | N.J.A.C. 13:45A-16.2(a)(7) |
| "Not a regulated home inspection" | N.J.S.A. 45:8-61 |
| CFA carve-out in liability limitation | N.J.S.A. 56:8-2 |
| Plain language | N.J.S.A. 56:12-14 |
| Mechanics' lien notice | N.J.S.A. 2A:44A-1 |

### Highest-Risk Areas
1. **Auto-renewal disclosure conspicuousness** — wrong font size = unenforceable renewal = pro-rata refund obligation per N.J.S.A. 56:12-16
2. **Use-it-or-lose-it on Concierge's 16 hours** — must be in plain language with member initial/checkbox; otherwise CFA omission claim
3. **Rate disclosure** — if member uses additional handyman hours, the $95/hr member rate + the after-hours $245/hr must both appear in this contract, not just on the invoice

### Estimated Effort
1 attorney-led drafting session (4–6 hours) + 1 revision pass.

## Agreement 2 — Snow Vendor Subcontractor Agreement

### Purpose
Convert the current handshake snow vendor relationship into a written prime/sub agreement with insurance flow-down, indemnification, lien waiver obligations, and independent contractor classification.

### Parties
- **Prime:** La Vaca General Contractors, LLC
- **Subcontractor:** Snow vendor (identity pending — Decision #7)

### Term Structure
Seasonal (typically October–April) with optional auto-renewal; either party may terminate with notice between seasons.

### Document Structure (Recommended Outline)
1. Parties, recitals, definitions
2. Scope of services: snow plowing, salting, sidewalk shoveling per property; trigger depth (typically 2"); response time SLAs; photo proof-of-service obligation
3. Property list (Exhibit A): each commercial property La Vaca services that requires snow coordination
4. Pricing: cost from sub to La Vaca; cadence (per event, per inch, seasonal flat, etc.)
5. **Markup acknowledgment**: sub acknowledges La Vaca will resell at a markup; no direct billing to La Vaca's clients
6. **Independent contractor classification**: ABC test compliance (N.J.S.A. 43:21-19) — explicit findings for parts B and C
7. **Insurance requirements**: sub maintains own CGL ($1M minimum), workers' comp for employees, commercial auto for plow trucks; La Vaca named as additional insured on CGL
8. **Insurance verification**: sub provides COI annually and at La Vaca's request; lapse = material breach
9. **Indemnification**: sub indemnifies La Vaca for sub's negligence, employees, equipment failure; flow-through to La Vaca's clients
10. **Lien waivers**: sub provides conditional/unconditional lien waivers upon payment
11. **Performance standards**: response time, completeness, photo quality; cure right; termination right
12. **Non-solicitation**: 1–2 years post-termination; sub cannot directly contract with La Vaca's commercial clients (confirm enforceability with NJ counsel)
13. **Compliance**: sub represents valid licensing, payroll tax compliance, anti-discrimination
14. **Dispute resolution**: NJ court, Essex County (or arbitration if preferred for B2B)
15. **Confidentiality**: client identity, pricing, contract terms
16. Force majeure
17. Notices, governing law, signatures

### Must-Have Clauses (NJ Statute Anchors)

| Clause | Statute |
|---|---|
| ABC test independent contractor classification | N.J.S.A. 43:21-19(i)(6)(A)(B)(C) |
| Sub's workers' comp coverage required | N.J.S.A. 34:15-1 |
| Sub's CGL + La Vaca as additional insured | Standard subcontract |
| Lien waiver flow-down | N.J.S.A. 2A:44A-1 |
| Markup transparency (CFA prophylaxis) | N.J.S.A. 56:8-1 |

### Highest-Risk Areas
1. **Misclassification under ABC test** — if sub doesn't pass parts B (work outside La Vaca's usual course of business) and C (sub is customarily engaged in an independent trade), the relationship is reclassified as employment, with payroll tax and workers' comp exposure
2. **Coverage gap on La Vaca's CGL** — confirm UFG knows snow coordination is part of the service line
3. **Statutory employer risk under N.J.S.A. 34:15-79** if sub lacks workers' comp

### Estimated Effort
1 attorney-led drafting session (2–4 hours). Snow vendor will likely want their own counsel review; budget for negotiation.

## Agreement 3 — Residential À La Carte Work Order

### Purpose
A per-visit binding scope-of-work and pricing document used for every standalone residential service (whether $99 detector check or $4,495 Premium Pre-Listing). Replaces the project-based CUST-contract for non-project work.

### Parties
- **Contractor:** La Vaca General Contractors, LLC
- **Customer:** Residential homeowner

### Term Structure
Single engagement; no renewal. Each work order is independently signed.

### Document Structure (Recommended Outline)
1. Header: HIC #, contractor info, customer info, property address, work order number
2. **NJ HIC notices** (3-day rescission, do-not-sign-in-blank, mechanics' lien) — same block as Membership Agreement and CUST-contract
3. **Service selected**: short scope statement + "What's Covered / What's NOT Covered" pulled from menu (or referenced as Exhibit A)
4. **Pricing**: flat fee, hourly rate, materials markup (retail + 25%), after-hours rate ($245/hr), member discount applied if applicable
5. **Scheduled visit date** and estimated duration
6. **Member status**: yes/no; if yes, member rate applied and link to active Membership Agreement
7. **Photo/video consent**: checkbox (for record-only use; marketing use requires separate release)
8. **Disclaimers** (applicable per service):
   - "NOT A REGULATED HOME INSPECTION" — always (covers Audit, Sump Test, property visits, Pre-Listing Walk)
   - "NOT AN ENVIRONMENTAL ASSESSMENT" — for any visual inspection service
   - "NOT ADA COMPLIANCE CERTIFICATION" — for Aging-in-Place
   - "NOT A LEGAL OPINION ON SECURITY DEPOSIT DEDUCTIBILITY" — for CCO prep and tenant documentation
9. **Warranty**: workmanship 30-day for repairs; no warranty for inspection-only services; manufacturer pass-through for installed components
10. **Limitation of liability** with CFA carve-out (model from CUST-contract Section 8)
11. **Materials**: provided by La Vaca at retail + 25%, or customer-supplied subject to Contractor approval (model from CUST-contract Section 2.6)
12. **Out-of-scope work**: handled by written Change Order at member or non-member hourly rate (with the rate clearly stated in this work order, not the CUST-contract's $350/hr)
13. **Payment terms**: due upon completion (or as agreed); card-on-file, check, Zelle; late fee per CUST-contract Section 3.4
14. **Cancellation by customer**: same 3-day right as Membership Agreement; cancellation between signing and visit per separate policy
15. **Dispute resolution**: NJ Essex County (consistent with Membership Agreement)
16. **Force majeure** (short form)
17. **Signatures** — contractor and customer

### Must-Have Clauses (NJ Statute Anchors)

| Clause | Statute |
|---|---|
| HIC # on face, contractor info | N.J.A.C. 13:45A-16.2(a)(1)–(3) |
| Written contract before work ($500+) | N.J.S.A. 56:8-151 |
| Scope of services and exclusions | N.J.A.C. 13:45A-16.2(a)(6) |
| Total price stated; all payment terms | N.J.A.C. 13:45A-16.2(a)(7) |
| 3-day rescission notice (bold/caps) | N.J.S.A. 56:8-152 |
| Do-not-sign-in-blank notice | N.J.A.C. 13:45A-16.2(a)(13) |
| Mechanics' lien notice | N.J.S.A. 2A:44A-1 |
| "Not a regulated home inspection" | N.J.S.A. 45:8-61 |
| Environmental / ADA disclaimers | CFA reliance risk |
| After-hours rate disclosed upfront | N.J.A.C. 13:45A-16.2(a)(7) |

### Highest-Risk Areas
1. **Rate consistency with Membership Agreement** — if a member books a one-off and the work order says non-member rate, that's a CFA misrepresentation; member status must be reflected in the work order
2. **Out-of-scope work pricing** — must use the published $95/$125/$245 rates, not the $350/hr from CUST-contract
3. **Inspection-only services need explicit "no repair, no warranty" framing** — otherwise a homeowner could argue La Vaca's failure to flag an issue triggers warranty obligations

### Estimated Effort
1 attorney-led drafting session (3–4 hours). This will be a reusable template with service-specific fill-in fields; the upfront effort pays off across hundreds of future engagements.

## Recommended Drafting Sequence

| Order | Agreement | Why this order |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Agreement 3 (À La Carte Work Order) | Highest reusability; locks in clause patterns (HIC notices, disclaimers, rate disclosure, warranty tiers) that will be reused in Agreements 1 and 4–14. |
| 2 | Agreement 1 (Membership Agreement) | Builds on Agreement 3's clause library; adds subscription/auto-renewal layer. Highest revenue impact. |
| 3 | Agreement 2 (Snow Subcontractor) | Independent of 1 and 3 (different parties, different statutes). Can be drafted in parallel if attorney capacity permits. |

Suggested parallel approach: Agreement 3 and Agreement 2 can be drafted concurrently (different statutory frameworks, no clause overlap). Agreement 1 should follow Agreement 3 since it inherits half its clauses.

## Decision Path

Two paths forward — pick one:

**Path A: Draft now, attorney-review later**
- Spawn the NJ contract validator agent to produce a first draft of each of the 3 agreements
- Iterate internally
- Then hand drafts + this plan + architecture brief to NJ attorney for review and execution
- **Pros:** faster to a tangible draft; attorney spends less time
- **Cons:** if attorney materially rewrites, drafting time was sunk cost; pre-drafting decisions (refund language, class waiver, e-sig platform) still must be resolved by attorney

**Path B: Brief attorney first, then draft**
- Send `legal/agreement-architecture.md` and this plan to NJ-licensed attorney
- Attorney returns: (a) confirmed statutory citations, (b) resolution of the 10 Open Decisions, (c) drafting kickoff
- **Pros:** every clause has attorney input from the start; lower risk of rework
- **Cons:** slower path to a usable draft; higher attorney bill (drafting + review vs. just review)

**Recommendation:** Path B. The 10 Open Decisions cannot be resolved without an attorney anyway (especially #4 class waiver, #6 refund policy, #9 referral handling, #5 UFG key-holder coverage). Drafting before those decisions are settled means rewriting the same clauses twice.
